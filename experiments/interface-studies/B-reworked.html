What this round does — and does not establish

  • B2 removes the 9% horizontal compression, strengthens strokes and redraws a, e, o, n, h, m, r, l, t, M and 1.
  • These studies retain some earlier project uppercase, digit and symbol drawings. They are revisions, not two wholly independent completed families.
  • Each has one regular style, 101 encoded characters, editable UFO/source drawings, individual SVGs and TTF/WOFF2 files.
  • Accents, full Latin Core, expanded kerning, hinting, multiple weights and cross-platform proofing are not complete.
  • Next: choose a direction, obtain a detailed type-design critique, refine individual outlines and spacing, then expand coverage and run technical checks. Passing checks will not establish Google's design approval.

B and B2 are working labels, not proposed public family names. Previous fonts and submissions are unchanged.
